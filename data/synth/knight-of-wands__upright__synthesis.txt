The Knight of Wands embodies the dialectical tension between departure and arrival, absence and presence. As a figure of movement and transfer, he navigates the space between the known and the unknown, traversing the distance between the familiar and the foreign. In this liminal state, he embodies the contradictions of the human experience, suspended between the certainties of home and the uncertainties of the open road, his fiery spirit burning with a fierce and unyielding resolve.
