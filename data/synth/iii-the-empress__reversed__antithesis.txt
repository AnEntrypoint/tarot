In the inverted III The Empress, the radiant light of truth is shrouded, its clarity obscured by the murkiness of obstruction. The public rejoicings that once accompanied her presence are now stifled, the bloom of her radiance stifled by the weight of constraint. The element of Water, typically associated with her, is now stagnant, its flow blocked by the opposing force of rigidity, the very thing that once brought her vitality and life, now a source of stagnation and decay.
