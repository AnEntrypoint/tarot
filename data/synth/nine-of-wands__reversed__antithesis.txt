In the Nine of Wands, the force of will and determination is stymied, its forward momentum halted by the weight of obstacles and adversity. The spark of the element Fire, which once burned with unyielding passion, now flickers dimly, its light diffused by the opacity of the world. The tension between the individual's drive and the external impediments that thwart it creates a sense of privation, a feeling of being trapped in a cycle of hardship and woe.
