In the Three of Cups, reversed, the impulse towards abundance and pleasure is stymied, its usual flow blocked by the constraints of the material world. The usual ease and comfort of this card, its promise of satisfaction and fulfillment, is now a distant memory, a fleeting dream of a life unlived. The dispatch of business and the execution of plans, once a straightforward affair, now stall and falter, leaving only a hollow sense of termination and loss.
