In the Two of Cups, reversed, the fluid, receptive energies of the suit are constricted, their flow impeded by the very desires and passions that once drove them. The yearning for union and connection now festers, a thwarted and unfulfilled longing that gnaws at the heart, a bitter taste of unrequited love or unmet expectation. The once-open vessel, now blocked, holds only the stagnant waters of disappointment and regret.
