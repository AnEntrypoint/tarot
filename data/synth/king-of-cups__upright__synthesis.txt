The King of Cups embodies a synthesis of opposing forces, a union of the rational and the emotional, the logical and the intuitive. This figure is a master of balance, a mediator between the realms of art and science, law and equity, with a deep understanding of the principles that govern human nature. He navigates the complexities of human relationships with a sense of responsibility and integrity, ever-vigilant against the insidious influences of ill-will and hypocrisy.
