In the dialectical dance of the tarot, VIII Strength embodies the antithesis, the opposing force that disrupts the harmony of the thesis. Here, the lion's unyielding will, a manifestation of the element Fire, is pitted against the gentle, almost ethereal beauty of the angel, a symbol of the divine. The tension between these two figures, one of unrelenting power and the other of serene strength, is the very essence of the card, a synthesis of opposing forces that cannot be reconciled, yet are inextricably linked.
