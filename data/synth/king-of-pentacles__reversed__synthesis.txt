The King of Pentacles, in his inverted state, embodies the perversity of a will turned inward, a corruption of the very principles that once guided him. His once-strong foundation, now weakened, is beset by the stench of his own defect, a misbehavior that has become a self-perpetuating cycle of ugliness. In this state, he is a man consumed by his own vices, his once-clear vision clouded by the mire of his own making.
