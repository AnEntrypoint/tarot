In the realm of Justice, the scales of balance are tipped precariously, as the opposing forces of rightness and wrongness are locked in a dialectical struggle. The upright figure of Justice, with its stern countenance and unyielding stance, embodies the unyielding principle of equity, yet its very presence is haunted by the specter of its opposite, the chaotic and arbitrary forces of chance and circumstance that threaten to upset the scales and render justice a mere abstraction.
