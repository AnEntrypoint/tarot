The King of Cups, in his inverted state, embodies the antithesis of his usual benevolent persona. His once-noble heart now festers with the stench of corruption and waste, his noble pursuits reduced to extortion and pillage. His position of power, once a symbol of authority, now serves as a platform for his own self-interest, his every action motivated by the desire to exploit and plunder, leaving only loss and devastation in his wake.
