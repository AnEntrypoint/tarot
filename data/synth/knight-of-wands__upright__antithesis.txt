The Knight of Wands embodies the dialectical tension of departure and arrival, a dynamic of dislocation and relocation that underscores the inherent contradictions of movement and rest. Waite's dark young man, driven by a restless energy, embodies the antithesis to the stasis of the status quo, his departure a necessary disruption to the monotony of existence. Etteilla's emphasis on sending away and abandonment underscores the Knight's role as a force of disruption, a Scorpio-Sagittarius conjunction that ignites the fire of change, a flame that burns away the old to make way for the new.
