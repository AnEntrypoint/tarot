In the crucible of the XV, the dialectical tension between force and fatality is resolved in a synthesis that absorbs both, a unity that reconfigures the oppositions of strength and violence. This card, a cipher of the earthy, Capricornian, and elemental, embodies the dialectical process of the universe, where the extraordinary efforts of human will are met with the unyielding fatality of destiny. The firmness of the earthy, the vehemence of the human, and the predestined course of events converge in a unity that is at once both the source and the outcome of the will to power.
