The Queen of Wands is a figure of practicality and industry, her earthy sensibilities rooted in the land and its rhythms. She is a woman of the earth, a farmer's wife or craftswoman who tends to the soil and its bounty, her love of money and success a byproduct of her hard work and resourcefulness. Her interest in others is often focused on those who share her down-to-earth values, and she is not one to be trifled with, her chaste and honourable nature tempered by a fierce and unyielding spirit.
