The Two of Swords embodies a paradoxical union of opposing forces, its harmony a fragile balance of conflicting elements. Waite notes that this card suggests both the conformity that comes with being in a state of arms, and the tenderness that arises from intimacy, yet these readings must be tempered by the card's generally negative connotations. Etteilla's emphasis on friendship and attachment underscores the card's potential for rapport and affinity, but also hints at the fragility of these bonds, as if they might shatter at any moment.
