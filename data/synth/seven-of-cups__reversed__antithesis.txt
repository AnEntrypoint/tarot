In the Seven of Cups, a figure is seen to be lost in the depths of their own desires, their willpower sapped by the weight of their own ambition. The determination that once drove them forward now falters, as the waters of their own imagination overflow with possibilities that cannot be grasped. The resolution that was once clear, now muddied by the undertow of their own unresolved longings, the figure is left to drown in the undertow of their own making.
