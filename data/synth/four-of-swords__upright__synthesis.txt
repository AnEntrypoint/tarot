In the Four of Swords, the figure of the hermit is situated in a state of vigilant repose, suspended between the stillness of solitude and the quietude of exile, as if poised between the tomb and the coffin, where the boundaries of self and world are blurred. This card is a synthesis of the contradictions that have been laid out before it, a resolution that absorbs the tension between the need for retreat and the need for engagement, a higher unity that re-frames the dichotomies of isolation and community.
