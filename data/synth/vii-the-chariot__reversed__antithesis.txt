In the inverted Chariot, the force of willful progress is stymied, its momentum stalled by the weight of its own pride and arrogance. The charioteer, once a paragon of self-assurance, now falters, its forward motion arrested by the very hubris that once drove it. The chariot, once a symbol of mastery over the self and the world, now lies still, its wheels spinning in futile circles, as the charioteer's vainglory and presumption have turned against it, rendering it impotent and self-destructive.
