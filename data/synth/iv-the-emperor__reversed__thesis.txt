In the inverted IV The Emperor, the benevolent ruler's authority is compromised, his influence stifled by the very forces he once wielded so effectively. His defense, once a bulwark against chaos, now falters, allowing the elements of disorder to seep in and undermine his shelter, his charity, his very sense of self. The fire that once burned within him, now reduced to smoldering embers, casts a faint glow on the ruins of his once-mighty edifice.
