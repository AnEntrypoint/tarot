In the Eight of Wands, a force once directed outward, now turns inward, its energy stifled by the weight of its own desires. A reversal of the usual trajectory, the Eight of Wands in this position is a harbinger of internal strife, as the passions and drives that once propelled the individual forward now constrict and corrode, like a fire burning in a confined space. The usual swiftness and decisiveness of the Eight of Wands, now reduced to a mere simmer, is instead a source of regret, remorse, and internal agitation.
