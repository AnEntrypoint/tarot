In the inverted stance of the Fool, we behold a figure both reckless and resigned, a synthesis of opposing forces that have been reduced to a nullity, a state of apathy that is not merely a lack of feeling, but a negation of all feeling, a privation of the vital energies that once drove the Fool's precipitous leap into the unknown. This is not a compromise, but a re-framing of the dialectical tension, a re-arrangement of the elements that once seemed so disparate, so that the Fool's original impetus is now turned inward, a self-referential loop of ineptitude and imprudence.
