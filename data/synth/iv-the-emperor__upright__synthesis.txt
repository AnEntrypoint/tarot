In the synthesis of the IV The Emperor, the opposing forces of reason and conviction are reconciled, their tension dissolving into a higher unity. This card represents the foundation upon which all else is built, the basis of stability and protection that underlies the structure of the self. As the element of Fire, it is the spark that ignites the will, the aid that fuels the journey towards realization, and the authority that guides the path towards the realization of one's true nature.
