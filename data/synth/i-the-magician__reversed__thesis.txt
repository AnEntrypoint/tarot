The Magician, in his inverted state, reveals a figure crippled by the very faculties he once wielded with effortless authority. His hands, once deftly crafting the threads of reality, now lie limp and still, as if paralyzed by the weight of his own mental machinations. In this state, the Magician's vaunted control over the elements of the universe has given way to a crippling anxiety, a disquiet that gnaws at his core, leaving him a hollow shell of his former self.
