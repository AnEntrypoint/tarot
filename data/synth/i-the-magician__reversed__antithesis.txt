In the inverted stance of the Magician, the adept's mastery of the arcane arts falters, their willpower crippled by the weight of their own anxieties. The Magus, once a master of the mental realm, is now beset by the very worries and disquietudes they once sought to vanquish, their mental acuity dulled by the opacity of their own self-doubt. The airy realm of the Magus, once a domain of effortless manifestation, is now a prison of stifled potential, the adept's inner turmoil a palpable force that blocks the flow of their will.
