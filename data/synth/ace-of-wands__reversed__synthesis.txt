In the inverted Ace of Wands, the primal force of creation and will is stunted, its radiance diffused, its spark dimmed. This is not a reversal of the card's fundamental nature, but rather a manifestation of its own self-contradiction, a collapse of the will into the abyss of its own desires. The clouded joy of the Ace's birth is now a ruin, a precipice where the self is lost in the depths of its own longing.
