In the reversed Knight of Wands, the fiery spark of passion and action is now smoldering, its flames reduced to a faint glow. This is not a card of resolution, but rather a card of obstruction, where the very forces that once drove towards union and creation are now turned inward, blocked by the weight of their own intensity. The reversal has transformed the Knight's forward momentum into a stifled, frustrated energy, one that longs for release, but cannot find it.
