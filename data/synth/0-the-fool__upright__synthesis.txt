The Fool, the first card of the Major Arcana, embodies the contradictions of the beginning: the precipice of reason and the abyss of unreason. Waite's words - folly, mania, extravagance - suggest a reckless abandon, a surrender to the void, while Etteilla's madness, insanity, and confusion imply a disconnection from the world. Yet, in the synthesis of these opposing forces, the Fool's innocence and enthusiasm become a kind of higher unity, an unbridled energy that dissolves the boundaries between reason and unreason, the known and the unknown.
