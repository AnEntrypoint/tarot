In the Seven of Pentacles, anxiety and worry writhe like a living thing, a palpable force that seizes the heart and refuses to let go. The weight of responsibility, the burden of expectation, the crushing pressure to perform - all these constricting forces congeal into a sense of spiritual torment, a feeling of being trapped in a labyrinth of one's own making. The earthy, material concerns that normally anchor us to the world now seem to have turned against us, blocking our path and stifling our potential, leaving us feeling drained, depleted, and defeated.
