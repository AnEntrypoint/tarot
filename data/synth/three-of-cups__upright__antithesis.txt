In the Three of Cups, we find the culmination of a process, a moment of perfect resolution, where the tensions of the journey are finally released. The card's imagery of three cups overflowing with abundance suggests a state of fulfillment, a happy issue, where the contradictions of the past are transcended. Yet, this triumph is not without its own contradictions, for in the midst of celebration, the military man, with his disciplined and practical nature, may find himself unexpectedly advanced, his rigidity yielding to the fluidity of emotional expression.
