The Eight of Wands is a card of unrelenting motion, a dynamo of activity that propels the individual forward with a sense of urgency and purpose, driven by a clear destination in sight, a felicitous end that promises satisfaction. This is a card of swiftness, of speed and haste, where the focus is on the path itself, the journey, rather than the destination, and where the arrows of love, in the form of passion and drive, fuel the forward motion.
