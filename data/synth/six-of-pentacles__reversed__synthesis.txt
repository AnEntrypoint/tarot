In the Six of Pentacles, the querent's desires, cupidity, and envy are turned inward, thwarting their own ambitions and aspirations. This reversal of the card's usual generosity and benevolence reveals a self-enclosed, self-interested individual, whose yearning for success is now a self-defeating force, blocking their own path to fulfillment. The once-promising career or pursuit, now stalled, reflects the querent's own inner limitations and weaknesses.
