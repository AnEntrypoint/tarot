In the Five of Swords, the antithesis of success and triumph, the querent's fortunes are beset on all sides by the ravages of defeat, their triumphs reduced to naught but the faintest glimmer of a memory, their very name besmirched by the stain of loss and dishonour. The element of Air, that most cerebral and rational of elements, is here reduced to a mere whisper, a hollow echo of what once was, as the querent's fortunes are ravaged by the opposing forces of destruction and degradation.
