In the synthesis of the Three of Pentacles, the tension between the practical and the grandiose is resolved in a union of the mundane and the sublime. The skilled labourer, the noble artisan, and the renowned figure are all absorbed into a higher unity, one that transcends the dichotomies of base and noble, of ordinary and extraordinary. This is not a compromise, but a re-framing that elevates the value of the everyday, the substantial, and the grand.
