In the King of Swords reversed, the rational faculties are mired in a morass of malevolence, their usual clarity and precision compromised by a toxic tangle of cruelty, perfidy, and inhumanity. This is not a figure of justice, but of injustice, a tyrannical force that seeks to crush the very principles of reason and compassion. The King's usual authority is undermined, his power turned inward, as he becomes a symbol of the very worst excesses of his own ego and ambition.
