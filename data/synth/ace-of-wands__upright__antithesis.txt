The Ace of Wands, a card of primal energy and unbridled potential, embodies the unyielding drive of creation and the unrelenting force of enterprise. It is the spark that sets the engine of progress in motion, the source from which all innovation and invention springs. Yet, it is also the harbinger of calamities, the catalyst for chaos and upheaval, as the unchecked power of the element of Fire can be a force of destruction, burning away the very foundations of stability and order.
