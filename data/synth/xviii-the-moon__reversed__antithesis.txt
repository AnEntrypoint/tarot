In the inverted XVIII The Moon, the fluid, dreamlike quality of the card is disrupted, its usual flow stymied by the blockage of its own potential. The watery, lunar force, typically associated with intuition and the subconscious, is now constricted, its usual adaptability turned against itself, creating a sense of stifled, anxious energy. The silences and deceptions that once lay hidden beneath the surface are now forced to the fore, like a deluge of unacknowledged fears and doubts.
