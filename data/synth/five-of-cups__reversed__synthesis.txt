In the Five of Cups, a figure stands at the threshold, gazing out at a scene of desolate beauty, the once-vibrant waters now stagnant and lifeless. The reversal of this card, a synthesis of the fragmented, suggests a return, not of a loved one, but of the self. The ancestral ties that bind us, the bloodlines that course through our veins, have been disrupted, their flow stunted by the weight of expectation and the false projects that have been foisted upon us.
