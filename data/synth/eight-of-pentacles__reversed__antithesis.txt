In the Eight of Pentacles, a once-promising career is now stunted by the weight of its own ambition, crippled by the insatiable hunger for more, more, more. The ingenious mind, once turned to cunning and intrigue, now finds itself ensnared in a web of usury, exaction, and extortion, its creative potential squandered by the relentless pursuit of advantage and augmentation. The earthy, practical concerns of the material world have become a prison, stifling the very skills that once promised so much.
