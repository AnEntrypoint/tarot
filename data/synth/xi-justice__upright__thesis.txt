In the realm of the law, XI Justice stands as the embodiment of rectitude, where the scales of rightness are balanced and weighed, the deserving side triumphs, and the scales of reason are brought to bear on the situation. The dialectical tension between justice and injustice is resolved through the application of equity, a fair and upright application of the law, unencumbered by personal bias or whimsy, guided instead by the principles of reason and the rectitude of the law itself.
