In the stark, unyielding presence of the Ten of Swords, the weight of suffering is palpable, a crushing burden that presses upon the heart like a physical force. Waite's words - pain, affliction, tears, sadness, desolation - distill the essence of this card, a condensation of the human experience of woe. Etteilla's litany of woes - sobs, whines, moans, grievances - underscores the cacophony of anguish that this card embodies, a symphony of sorrow that refuses to be stilled.
