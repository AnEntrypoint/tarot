In the Six of Wands (reversed), the triumphant procession of the upright figure, now falters, its forward momentum stalled by the weight of its own expectations. The gates of victory, once open to the promise of success, now creak and groan under the pressure of treacherous delay, as the very forces that once propelled the hero forward now turn inward, suffocating the will to act, and leaving only the bitter taste of apprehension and fear.
