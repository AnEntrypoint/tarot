The situation is one of stagnation, a game of chance turned against itself, where fortune and misfortune are inextricably linked, and the outcome is as much a matter of fate as it is of decision. In this state, the usual certainties of wealth and security are turned inward, their value diminished by the uncertainty that surrounds them, and the only true prize is the ability to adapt to the whims of chance, to navigate the labyrinthine paths of fate.
