In the inverted, petrified state of XIII Death, the force of transformation is stymied, its energies turned inward, its dynamism arrested by the weight of its own inertia. Like the sleep of the somnambulant, or the paralysis of the null, the reversed Death card suggests a state of suspended animation, a condition in which the very forces that once drove the individual forward are now stilled, frozen in a state of nullity, a negation of the self.
