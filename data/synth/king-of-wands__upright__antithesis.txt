The King of Wands embodies a paradoxical figure, both rustic and refined, his rugged individualism tempered by a sense of civic duty. He is a farmer, a grower, a tiller of the earth, yet also a man of discerning taste, with a keen sense of justice and a strong moral compass. His fiery nature is both creative and destructive, capable of sparking innovation and progress, yet also prone to impulsive action and reckless abandon.
