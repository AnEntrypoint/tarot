In the inverted stance of the Magician, the adept's mastery of the elements is stymied, their willpower crippled by the weight of their own anxieties. The airy realm of mental clarity, once a domain of effortless creation, is now a battleground of worry and disquiet, the mind's own dissonance a palpable force that hinders the adept's ability to manifest their will. The Magician's inner turmoil is a self-imposed exile from the unity of their own being, a disconnection from the higher synthesis that once flowed through them.
