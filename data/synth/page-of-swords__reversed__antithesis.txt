In the inverted state of the Page of Swords, the unbridled flow of ideas and information is stymied, its sharp edges dulled by a lack of foresight and preparation. The astonishing news that once brought such clarity and insight now brings only confusion and disarray, as the mind is overwhelmed by the unpreparedness that has befallen it. The earthy, grounded quality of the Page, typically associated with the practical and the rational, is here turned inward, as the individual is forced to confront the unexamined assumptions and unchallenged certainties that have led to this state of disarray.
