In the inverted light of XVII The Star, the celestial harmony is disrupted, its gentle radiance obscured by the murkiness of the material world. The music of the spheres, once a sweet and effortless melody, is now a cacophony of conflicting desires and ambitions, each one a shrill and self-interested cry that drowns out the song of the universe. The stars, once a guiding light, are now a distant and fading memory, their gentle influence stymied by the turbulent winds of human ego and will.
