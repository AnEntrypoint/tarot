In the Five of Wands, the spark of conflict is doused, its flames reduced to smoldering embers. The opposing forces, once locked in a fierce struggle, now grapple with a sense of stalemate, their energies spent and their wills exhausted. The Five of Wands in the reversed position, however, presents a different picture: not the absence of strife, but its stifling presence, a festering wound that refuses to heal, a constant reminder of the unresolved tensions that simmer just beneath the surface, waiting to erupt once more.
