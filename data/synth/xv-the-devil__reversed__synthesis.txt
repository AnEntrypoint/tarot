In the inverted state of XV The Devil, the fetid energies of darkness and despair are turned inward, their malevolent force now directed against the self. This is not a liberation from the abyss, but rather a self-imprisonment, a confinement to the very depths of one's own weakness and pettiness. The light of reason and understanding, symbolized by the Capricornian horns that crown the figure, is now obscured, plunging the individual into a state of despondency and faint-heartedness, where the only horizon is the bleak landscape of one's own inner desolation.
