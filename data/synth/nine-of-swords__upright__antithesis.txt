In the Nine of Swords, the figure of the ecclesiastic, a symbol of piety and devotion, is beset on all sides by the weight of failure and disappointment, their piety turned to dust by the cruel winds of circumstance. The card's association with Mars in Gemini, the planet of conflict and the element of Air, underscores the intellectual and emotional turmoil that arises from the contradictions of the self, the individual's own thoughts and desires turned against them like a maelstrom of sharp and cutting words.
