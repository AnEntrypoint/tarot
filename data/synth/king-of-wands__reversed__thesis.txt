The King of Wands, in his reversed position, bespeaks a force now turned inward, its austerity and severity redirected against itself, a self-criticism that undermines its own potency. This is not a surrender, but a reorientation, a recalibration of the will, as the King's indulgence and tolerance, once directed outward, now confront the self, a dialectical reversal that seeks to understand the contradictions within.
