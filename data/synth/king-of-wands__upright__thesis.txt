The King of Wands embodies a sturdy, earthy figure, a rustic dweller who tills the land with conscientious care, his honesty and integrity as solid as the soil he cultivates. This down-to-earth individual, often married and settled, is a dependable presence, his practicality and sense of duty unwavering. His is a favourable influence, a good omen for a secure and stable future, one that promises a fruitful harvest of one's labors.
