In the Four of Pentacles, the stabilizing force of the material world is in disarray, its usual certainties disrupted by the constraints of circumstance. The solidity of the earthy element, once a foundation for stability, is now constricted, its potentialities pent up, its usual flow arrested by the very determinations that once defined it. The result is a stifled, inward-turning energy, a thwarted expression of the will, its usual expansive reach curtailed by the limits imposed upon it.
