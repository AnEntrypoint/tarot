The Knight of Pentacles, in his reversed position, embodies the contradictions of a force that has been stymied, its dynamism turned inward, its usual sense of purpose and drive now stifled by the very inertia and idleness it once sought to overcome. In this state, the Knight's usual stalwartness has given way to a kind of apathy, a numbness that threatens to consume him, rendering him a shadow of his former self, a brave man out of employment, lost in a sea of stagnation and despondency.
