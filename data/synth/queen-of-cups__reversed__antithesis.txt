The Queen of Cups, in her inverted state, embodies the contradictions of a woman whose reputation for virtue is belied by her true nature, a dichotomy that renders her a force to be reckoned with, yet one whose influence is ultimately self-destructive. Her once-dignified position is now undermined by a propensity for vice and dishonour, her waters turned against themselves in a self-sabotaging cycle of corruption and scandal.
