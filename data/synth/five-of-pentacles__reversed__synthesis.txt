In the Five of Pentacles, disorder and chaos reign, a muddled state of affairs that bespeaks a lack of order and a disorganized, clashing, and discordant world. This is not a state of being, but a state of becoming, a moment of arrested development, where the usual structures and certainties of the material realm are disrupted, and the usual flows of energy are blocked or turned inward, creating a sense of ruin and dissipation.
