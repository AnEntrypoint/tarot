The Ace of Swords cuts through the tumult of excess, its sharp edge slicing through the chaos of extreme passion and fury, revealing the clear-eyed logic of reason. In this moment of triumph, the card's force is both creative and destructive, capable of unleashing great prosperity or great misery, its power a double-edged sword that cuts both ways. As the root of Air, it stands as the unifying principle, dissolving the oppositions that have been forged in the crucible of excess, and revealing the clear, unadulterated truth.
