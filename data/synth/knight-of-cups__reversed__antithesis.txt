The Knight of Cups, in his inverted state, embodies the duplicitous nature of the rational, his actions now tainted by the very artifices he once employed with such finesse. His subtlety, once a hallmark of his cunning, has curdled into a self-serving duplicity, a ruse that turns inward, crippling his own momentum. In this state, he is a master of the subtle, yet his own guile has become a liability, a weight that blocks his progress.
