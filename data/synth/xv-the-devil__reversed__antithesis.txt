In the inverted realm of XV The Devil, the abyssal darkness of self-deception and self-delusion is revealed, a void that swallows all light and reason. The petty, weak, and faint-hearted are consumed by their own despair, their capacity for tenderness and compassion turned against themselves, a self-devouring that is both pitiless and pitiful. The earthy, material concerns that once anchored them to reality now suffocate, as the weight of their own self-doubt crushes them beneath the unyielding stone of their own making.
