The Wheel of Fortune, in its inverted state, bespeaks a stagnation of growth, a stultification of abundance. The augmentation that once promised increase and development now falters, its energies turned inward, its potential squandered, its promise of superfluity unfulfilled. In this state, the very forces that once drove expansion and progress now constrict and restrict, stifling the very growth they once fostered, leaving only a hollow, barren husk in their wake.
