In the Ten of Cups reversed, the tranquil surface of the cup, once a vessel for the purest of emotions, is now a vessel for the turbulent and the tormented. The waters that once flowed smoothly, now churn and boil, reflecting the inner turmoil that has taken hold. Waite's "indignation" and Etteilla's "fury" and "rage" seethe beneath the surface, as the cup, once a symbol of joy and fulfillment, is now a cauldron of bitter resentment and unfulfilled desire.
