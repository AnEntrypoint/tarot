The Three of Wands embodies the synthesis of initiative and established strength, where boldness and daring are tempered by a keen sense of collaboration and mutual support. It is the card of the merchant prince, whose ships sail across the horizon, bearing the fruits of his enterprise, and whose gaze is cast towards others with a view to sharing his success. In this union of opposing forces, the tension between starting anew and consolidating gains dissolves, yielding a higher unity that absorbs both, a virtuous and established strength that is the hallmark of true leadership.
