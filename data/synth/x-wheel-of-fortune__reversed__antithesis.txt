In the Wheel of Fortune, the axis of destiny is twisted, its cyclical motion stalling, its promise of increase and abundance now a hollow mockery. The augmentation of fortune, once a boundless wellspring, has shrunk to a trickle, its growth stifled by the weight of its own expectations. The fire of Jupiter, once a blaze of possibility, now flickers with a faint, desperate spark, its light struggling to pierce the darkness that surrounds it.
