In the inverted, petrified state of XIII Death, the forces of inertia and nullity converge, rendering the individual paralyzed, asleep, and disconnected from the world. This is not a state of rest, but rather a state of arrested motion, a stasis that freezes the will and numbs the senses. The usual rhythms of life, the pulsing currents of vitality, are stilled, leaving only a dull, leaden weight that presses upon the heart, a somnambulant heaviness that threatens to annihilate all hope.
