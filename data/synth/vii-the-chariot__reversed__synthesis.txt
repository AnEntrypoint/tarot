In the reversed VII Chariot, the force of progress and willpower is stymied, its forward momentum halted by the weight of its own arrogance. The charioteer, once a symbol of mastery and control, now falters under the burden of its own pride, its vainglorious ambitions deflated by the crushing weight of its own presumption. The fluid, watery element that once buoyed its forward motion is now congealed, its flow blocked by the icy grip of its own self-importance.
