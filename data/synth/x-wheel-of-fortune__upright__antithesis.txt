In the Wheel of Fortune, the dialectical tension between the cyclical nature of destiny and the capricious whims of luck is palpable. Waite's association of the card with success and elevation belies the crushing weight of fate's indifference, while Etteilla's emphasis on fortune and happiness glosses over the abyss of uncertainty that lies beneath. Crowley's elemental fire, symbolized by the expansive, all-consuming Jupiter, underscores the unrelenting force of destiny's wheel, which turns with an unyielding logic that brooks no opposition.
