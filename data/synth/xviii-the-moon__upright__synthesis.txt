In the stillness of the Moon, the dialectical tension between the forces of light and darkness is resolved, as the fluid, adaptable will of the self is re-framed, its aim and intention now a synthesis of opposing elements, the watery depths of the psyche a crucible for the alchemical transformation of error into wisdom, the language of the self a dialectical dance of truth and deception, the resolution a unity that absorbs the contradictions of the self and the world.
