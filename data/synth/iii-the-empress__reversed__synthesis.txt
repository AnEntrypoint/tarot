In the inverted III The Empress, the radiant light of truth is shrouded, its clarity obscured by the murk of self-doubt and vacillation. The element of Water, typically associated with emotional and intuitive depths, is instead constricted, its flow stunted by the weight of indecision. The Empress, once a symbol of public rejoicings and celebrations, now languishes in private, her inner world darkened by the absence of clarity.
