The Three of Wands embodies the figure of a merchant prince, standing at the helm of his ship, gazing out at the horizon with a sense of established strength and enterprise. His vessels, bearing the weight of his wares, are poised on the cusp of discovery, their sails billowing with the wind of initiative, as if ready to set sail into the unknown. This is a card of collaboration, of partnership, where the lines of commerce and trade are drawn between individuals, each one looking to the other with a view to mutual aid and support.
