In the synthesis of opposites, Temperance embodies the dialectical resolution of excess and restraint, its moderation a product of the reconciliation between the opposing forces of economy and extravagance, frugality and indulgence, management and abandon. As a figure of balance, Temperance stands at the intersection of the fiery, expansive element of Sagittarius, and the earthy, stabilizing forces of the material world, its very being a testament to the power of accommodation and the integration of disparate elements.
