In the inverted realm of the High Priestess, the normally inscrutable veil of intuition is rent asunder, revealing a garden of stifled passions and thwarted desires, where the effervescent waters of the subconscious have become stagnant and still, their bubbling fervor turned to a dull, muddy pool, as the self's surface-level knowledge and conceit are stripped away, leaving only a hollow, gnarled shell of a once-vibrant, once-vital force, now reduced to a mere husk of its former, unbridled self.
