In the Six of Pentacles, the present moment is a crucible of opportunity and responsibility, where the weight of past and future is momentarily suspended. The card's emphasis on the here and now, as Etteilla's title "Six of Coins" suggests, is a call to attention, a vigilance that must be cultivated in order to reap the rewards of the present moment. Waite's notes on the card's themes of presents, gifts, and gratification underscore the idea that the present is a time of exchange, where the energies of past and future are distilled into a moment of clarity and resolution, a synthesis that dissolves the tension between the two.
