In the inverted stance of the Hierophant, the figure of the traditional sage is reduced to a hollow shell of its former self, a shell that once cradled the very wisdom and knowledge it now vainly attempts to convey. The once-steady hand that once guided the seeker through the labyrinth of life now falters, its grasp on the philosopher's stone of understanding tenuous at best, its words a jumbled mess of over-kindness and weakness.
