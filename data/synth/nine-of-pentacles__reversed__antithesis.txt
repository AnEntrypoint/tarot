In the Nine of Pentacles, the earthy solidity of the material world is undercut by a sense of duplicity, as if the very ground beneath one's feet is sullied by the weight of deceit. The inverted, reversed figure of the Nine of Pentacles appears to be engaged in a ruse, a trickery that belies its own solidity, a Venus in Virgo, earth bound yet unmoored from its own integrity. The promise of gain, of stability, is here revealed as a swindle, a cheat, a hollow promise that betrays its own earthy substance.
