In the Five of Swords, the weight of sorrow and mourning is palpable, a burden that presses upon the heart like a physical force. The reversal of this card, a sign of defeat and desolation, suggests a turning inward of the same energies, a stifling of the vital spark that once animated the individual. In this state, the aspirant is beset on all sides by the very same forces that once fueled their triumph, now turned against them with a crushing, suffocating weight.
