In the Wheel of Fortune, the dialectical synthesis of opposing forces resolves into a higher unity, a turning of the wheel that sublates the tension between fate and free will, success and failure, elevation and decline. As the card's elemental affinity with fire suggests, this is a moment of ignition, a spark that sets the wheel in motion, propelling the individual towards a trajectory of fortune, where luck and happiness converge with improvement and prosperity, dissolving the distinctions between fate and choice, and resolving the contradictions of the lower levels into a unified, ascending whole.
