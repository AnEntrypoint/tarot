In the Ten of Cups reversed, the tranquil surface of the cup, once a symbol of fulfillment and contentment, is shattered by the turmoil that lies beneath. The waters that once flowed smoothly, now churn with anger, indignation, and fury, as the contradictions of the self are forced to the fore. The very essence of the cup, once a vessel of joy, is now a cauldron of bitter resentment, its contents boiling over with a violence that threatens to spill out and engulf all in its path.
