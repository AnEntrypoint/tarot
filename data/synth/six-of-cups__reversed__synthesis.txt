In the Six of Cups, reversed, the fluid, dreamy potential of the future is stymied, its promise of renewal and inheritance stalled, its forward momentum arrested. The pleasure of the Sun in Scorpio, now a faint, flickering flame, is suffocated by the weight of what's come before, its vitality turned inward, its life force constricted. The usual flow of time, the unfolding of events, is blocked, its course altered, its trajectory twisted, its forward-looking gaze turned back upon itself.
