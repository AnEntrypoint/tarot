In the synthesis of XXI, the World, the dialectical tension between the opposing forces of Saturn and Earth dissolves, yielding a higher unity that absorbs both. This is not a compromise, but a re-framing that resolves the dichotomies of voyage and rest, movement and stasis, change and fixity. The World, as the ultimate synthesis, embodies the assured success and recompense of the journey, the pilgrimage of the soul, and the flight of the spirit, all grounded in the element of Earth, the physical and material realm that anchors the aspirant to the terrestrial plane.
