The Knight of Pentacles embodies a utilitarian ethos, driven by a practical and serviceable approach to life. His focus on usefulness and advantage is tempered by a sense of responsibility, a rectitude that guides his actions towards the greater good. In the realm of the material world, he navigates with a keen eye for profit and interest, ever seeking to better his circumstances and those of others, his officious nature a double-edged sword of duty and self-interest.
