In the reversed Page of Wands, the spark of creativity and initiative, typically so eager to burst forth, is instead stifled, its flame flickering dimly, struggling to find purchase in a world that seems to have lost its impetus. This is not a card of pure negation, but rather a card of arrested potential, a warning that the impetus for action, for change, for growth, is being held back, blocked by some unseen force, a lesson unlearned, a teaching untaught. The earthy, grounded quality of the fire element, typically so buoyant and expansive, now feels heavy, weighed down, as if the very ground itself is a physical, crushing force.
