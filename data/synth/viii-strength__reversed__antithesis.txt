In the inverted VIII Strength, the majestic lion's ferocity is stifled, its fiery essence constricted by the weight of its own authority, its dominion over itself a suffocating burden. The sovereign's reign is reduced to a stifling despotism, its empire crumbling under the weight of its own weakness, its administration mired in discord and disarray, its authority turned inward, a self-consuming force that eats away at its own foundations.
