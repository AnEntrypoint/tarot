The Magician's adeptness is a masterful blend of skill, diplomacy, and address, a trifecta of qualities that bespeak a mastery of the subtleties of human interaction, yet also a vulnerability to the sickness of others, the pain of loss, and the disaster of misjudgment. His self-confidence, a will to act, is both a strength and a weakness, a double-edged sword that cuts both ways, as the querent, if male, navigates the complexities of his own psyche, beset on all sides by the vicissitudes of life.
