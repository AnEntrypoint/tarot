In the Six of Cups, the past and its attendant memories loom large, a nostalgic longing for what has vanished, like the withered blooms of a once-vibrant garden. Yet, this card also holds the potential for new relations and knowledge, a reversal of fortunes that upends the stodgy certainties of age, allowing the children to frolic in unfamiliar precincts, their laughter and playfulness unencumbered by the weight of what has been.
