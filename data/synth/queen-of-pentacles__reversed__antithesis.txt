In the Queen of Pentacles, the practical, material world is turned on its head, its usual certainties upended. The Queen, once a paragon of stability and security, now falters, her usual aplomb shaken by the very doubts and fears that once fueled her ambitions. Her once-clear vision is clouded, her usually steady hand now hesitant, as the weight of uncertainty and indecision bears down upon her, threatening to undermine the very foundations of her being.
