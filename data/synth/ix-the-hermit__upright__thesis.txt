In the stillness of the Hermit, prudence and circumspection reign, a watchful eye cast over the realm of appearances, where treason and dissimulation lurk, masquerading as virtue, their hypocritical guises a testament to the ease with which the self can be duped, the lines between truth and falsehood blurred, the element of earth, solid and unyielding, a reminder that even the most well-intentioned hearts can be corrupted by the weight of their own deceit.
