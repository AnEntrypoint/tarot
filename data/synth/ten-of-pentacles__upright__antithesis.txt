The Ten of Pentacles, a card of gain and riches, stands as the antithesis to the fluid, adaptive nature of the Fool, its opposite number. While the Fool is the spark of potential, the Ten of Pentacles is the fixed, materialized outcome of that potential, the culmination of effort and investment. It is the house that has been built, the family that has been established, the wealth that has been accumulated, a monument to the power of industry and thrift.
