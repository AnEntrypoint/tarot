In the Four of Wands, reversed, we find the antithesis of completion, a state of stagnation and frustration that arises from the very success it once promised. The augmentation and increase of Waite and Etteilla's descriptions are now blocked, the advancement and happiness stifled by the weight of one's own desires, which have become self-perpetuating and self-destructive, like the fire of the element that once fueled them, now reduced to smoldering embers.
