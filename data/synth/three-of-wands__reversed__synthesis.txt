In the Three of Wands, the triumphant figure stands poised on the precipice of a new era, their strength and willpower momentarily stymied by the weight of their own unfulfilled ambitions. The reversal of this card, then, is not a negation of its virtues, but rather a testament to the exhaustion of its energies, a collapse of the will that once drove the aspirant forward. The spark of fire that once fueled their endeavors now smolders, a spent force that flickers with a faint, desperate light.
