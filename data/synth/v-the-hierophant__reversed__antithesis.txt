In the inverted Vau of the Hierophant, the once-steadfast sage is now crippled by its own dogmatic certainties, its philosophical rigor turned inward to a self-referential, self-repulsive cycle of doubt and confusion. The earthy, dependable Taurus, once the rock upon which the Hierophant's wisdom was built, now lies barren and unfruitful, a sterile landscape of unfulfilled potential.
