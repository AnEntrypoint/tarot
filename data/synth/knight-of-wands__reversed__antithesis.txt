The Knight of Wands, in its reversed position, embodies the antithesis of its usual fiery and impulsive nature, now stifled and turned inward, its passion and drive reduced to a smoldering ember. This is not a card of resolution, but of obstruction, its usual path forward blocked by some unseen force, its energy pent up and frustrated, like a woman's desires, perhaps, in a loveless marriage. The spark that once drove it forward now flickers with a faint, resentful glow, a spark that refuses to be extinguished, but cannot be fanned into flame.
