In the dialectical dance of the tarot, XVII The Star stands as the antithesis to the preceding card, the Lovers, with its promise of union and harmony. Here, the very fabric of reality is stripped bare, leaving only the stark, unforgiving light of the void. The theft of meaning, the stripping away of certainties, is a painful process, one that requires a reckoning with the abyss of uncertainty, where the only constants are the shifting sands of separation and the cold wind of change.
