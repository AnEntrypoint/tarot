In the King of Wands in reverse, the fiery dynamism of the suit is stymied, its force turned inward, constricted by the constraints of the querent's circumstances. This is not a diminishment of the King's power, but rather a rechanneling of it, a redirection of the will towards more subtle, more nuanced expression. The King's severity, once a necessary counterbalance to his indulgence, now becomes a self-imposed prison, a blockage that stifles the very energy it once unleashed.
