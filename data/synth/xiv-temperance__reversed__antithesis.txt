In the inverted realm of Temperance, the fluid, harmonizing force of balance and moderation is disrupted, its gentle flow stymied by the opposing weight of rigidity and dogma. The priest, once a unifying figure, now stands as a symbol of division, a mediator of competing interests that cannot be reconciled, a church torn asunder by its own conflicting sects and denominations. The fire of conviction burns cold and dark, its warmth extinguished by the chill of unyielding doctrine.
