The Ten of Pentacles, in its reversed position, bespeaks a force maelstromed by the capricious whims of chance, its usual benevolence turned to malevolence, its gifts and dowries reduced to mere scraps, its games of hazard now games of survival, where the element of earth, once fertile and generative, is now barren and unforgiving. The usual certainties of fortune and fate are here upended, leaving only the stark, unvarnished reality of loss and privation.
