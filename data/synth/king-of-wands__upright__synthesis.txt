The King of Wands embodies a sturdy, earthy pragmatism, a rootedness in the land and its rhythms, yet also an unshakeable sense of justice and a keen moral compass. This is a figure of unyielding integrity, a man of the soil who knows his place in the world and holds fast to his values, yet is not averse to taking bold action when the situation demands it. His is a vision of a life lived in harmony with the land and with others, where the cycles of nature are respected and the fruits of one's labor are a source of pride and satisfaction.
