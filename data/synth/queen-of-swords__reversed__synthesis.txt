The Queen of Swords, in her reversed position, is a figure of malice and deceit, her usual sharpness turned inward, mired in the very artifices she once wielded with such precision. Her once-clear vision is now clouded by her own prudery, her judgments warped by a subtle but insidious bigotry that blinds her to the truth. In this state, she is a force to be reckoned with, but one that is ultimately self-destructive, her own malice turned against herself.
