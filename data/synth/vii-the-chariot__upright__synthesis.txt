In the synthesis of VII The Chariot, the dialectical tension between opposing forces is resolved through the triumph of the will, as the charioteer navigates the waters of life with a sense of providence, harnessing the energies of war and triumph to overcome the troubles that arise from dissension and discord. The charioteer's journey is not one of compromise, but of re-framing, where the contradictions of the past are sublated, and a new unity emerges, unencumbered by the burdens of presumption and vengeance.
