In the Ten of Swords, the triumphant figure, once a paragon of success and authority, now lies broken and defeated, its power and influence spent. The reversal of fortune has turned its strength into weakness, its advantage into disadvantage, its empire into ruin. The once-mighty warrior, now crippled and impotent, is forced to confront the abyss of its own limitations, its vaunted authority reduced to a mere shadow of its former self.
