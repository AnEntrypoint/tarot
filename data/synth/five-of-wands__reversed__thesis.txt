In the Five of Wands, a reversal of fortunes, the strife that once fueled the passions of individuals now stagnates, its fire reduced to a smoldering ember, a spent force that can no longer be harnessed for progress. The quarrels that once promised advantage have turned to bitter recrimination, the delaying tactics of a bygone era now a self-imposed prison, a cycle of obstruction that stifles the very momentum it once claimed to foster.
