The Ten of Cups in its reversed position bespeaks a state of stagnation, a repressed and festering discontent that refuses to be assuaged. This is not a calm and peaceful scene, but one of simmering anger and frustration, a pent-up fury that threatens to erupt at any moment, a maelstrom of hurt and resentment that cannot be contained. The usual associations with this card, of joy and fulfillment, are here turned on their head, revealing a darker, more troubled reality.
