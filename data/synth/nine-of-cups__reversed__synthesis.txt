In the Nine of Cups, the fluid, receptive energies of the suit are turned against themselves, their usual flow and abundance now constricted and stagnant. The usual virtues of sincerity, truth, and loyalty, which in their upright position suggest a harmonious integration of the self and the world, are here blocked and inverted, suggesting a disconnection from the very sources of happiness and fulfillment that the card is meant to represent. The result is a sense of inner turmoil, a feeling of being trapped in a sea of one's own desires and emotions, with no clear escape or resolution.
