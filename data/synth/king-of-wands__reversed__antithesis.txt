The King of Wands, in his reversed stance, embodies a paradoxical blend of indulgence and condescension, a severity tempered by a tolerant air that belies a deeper unease. His once-steadfast resolve now falters, his inner fire smothered by an oppressive weight, his grandeur reduced to a mere shadow of its former self. In this state, his indulgence turns inward, fueling a self-consuming passion that eats away at his very foundations.
