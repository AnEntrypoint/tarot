In the inverted light of XVII The Star, the celestial harmony that once shone so brightly now falters, its music reduced to a discordant hum, its beauty tarnished by the weight of its own pretensions. The star's gentle lapping of the waves has given way to a stagnant pool, its waters choked with the detritus of our own hubris, our arrogance and haughtiness now a self-imposed prison that stifles the very life force it once sought to embody.
