In the King of Pentacles, a figure of unyielding resolve, the once-sturdy edifice of authority crumbles, its foundations sapped by the insidious forces of corruption and vice. The King's grip on the world tightens, but his hold on himself falters, as the weight of his own debauchery and misbehavior threatens to consume him, rendering him a shadow of his former self, a perverted and deformed monarch, crippled by his own weaknesses.
