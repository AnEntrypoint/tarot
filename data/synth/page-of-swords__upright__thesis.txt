The Page of Swords embodies the vigilant and watchful quality of the element of Air, situated in the realm of the rational and the discerning, where one's faculties are sharpened to observe and examine the minutest details, ever on the lookout for the hidden patterns and underlying dynamics that govern the world. This card is the quintessential spy, connoisseur, and steward, always on the prowl for the secrets that lie beneath the surface, ever prepared to note and remark on the subtleties of the human condition.
