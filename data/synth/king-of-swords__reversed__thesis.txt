The King of Swords, in his inverted form, bespeaks a malevolence that has turned in upon itself, a perversity of will that has become its own ruin. His once-noble authority now falters, crippled by the very cruelty he once wielded with such precision, his malice turned inward, a self-devouring force that eats away at his own foundations, leaving him a shadow of his former self, a hollow shell of a once-just ruler.
