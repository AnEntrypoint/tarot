The Page of Wands, in its reversed position, embodies the antithesis of its usual fiery and impetuous nature, its energies now stifled and turned inward, as if the very spark that once drove it to action has been extinguished, leaving only a smoldering ember of frustration and indecision. This is not a card of bold announcements and declarations, but rather a card of unfulfilled potential, its usual confidence and assertiveness replaced by a sense of uncertainty and doubt, its usual passion and creativity now blocked and stifled, unable to find expression.
