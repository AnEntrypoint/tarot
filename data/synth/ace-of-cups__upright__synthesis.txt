The Ace of Cups, a card of unshakeable abundance, presents a tableau of the heart's unyielding will, a table spread before us like the Holy Table of Waite's mystical vision, where every guest is welcomed with an unalterable feast of joy and nourishment. This is no fleeting pleasure, but a fundamental law, an inflexible principle that governs the flow of life, a root of the element Water, the very source of all fertility and growth.
