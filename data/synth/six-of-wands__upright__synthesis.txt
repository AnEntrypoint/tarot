The Six of Wands is a card of triumphant resolution, where the tension between opposing forces is transcended, and a higher unity is achieved. On the surface, it appears as a victor, crowned with laurels, but beneath, it conceals a complex web of relationships, where even the most seemingly secure positions can be undermined by the treachery of those who serve. The card's multiple significations - news, hope, triumph - are inextricably linked, as the desire for success is inextricably bound to the fear of loss, and the servant is both the enabler and the obstacle to the master's ambition.
