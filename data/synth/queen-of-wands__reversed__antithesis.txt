In the Queen of Wands' reversed position, the fiery spark of her will is smothered, her generosity turned inward, a cauldron of unfulfilled potential. Waite's description of opposition, jealousy, and even deceit suggests a force that's been turned against itself, a self-sabotaging energy that's more likely to scald than enflame. The querent's good intentions, once directed outward, now fester and stagnate, a watered-down version of the original flame.
