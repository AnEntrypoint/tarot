The Knight of Cups embodies the dialectical tension between the certainties of the rational and the mysteries of the emotional, a meeting of opposing forces that cannot be reconciled. Waite's description of the Knight as an "arrival, approach" suggests a precipitous moment, a sudden convergence of disparate elements, while Etteilla's emphasis on "coming together" and "meeting" underscores the Knight's role as a catalyst for union and agreement. This card is the antithesis, the opposing force that disrupts the stasis of the rational, bringing the Querent into contact with the unknown, the uncharted territories of the emotional realm.
