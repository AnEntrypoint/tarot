In the inverted Ace of Swords, the sharp edge of reason and intellect is dulled, its cutting edge blunted by the weight of its own rigidity. The clarity of thought, once a beacon of truth, now falters, its vision clouded by the opacity of its own self-certainty. The sterile, unyielding logic that once ruled with an iron fist now succumbs to the messy, uncharted territories of emotion and intuition, its precision and control giving way to a crippling indecisiveness.
