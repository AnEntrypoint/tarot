The King of Swords embodies the dialectical synthesis of reason and authority, a synthesis that is both pragmatic and principled. He is the embodiment of the law, a figure of unyielding judgment and unshakeable conviction, yet also a master of the subtle nuances of argument and persuasion. His authority is not a blunt instrument, but a finely honed tool of discernment, wielded with precision and calculation, ever-vigilant for the contradictions that threaten the order he seeks to uphold.
