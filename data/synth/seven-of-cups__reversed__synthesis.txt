In the Seven of Cups, a force of will and determination is turned inward, its usual expansive and creative potential stymied by a sense of blockage or obstruction. This is not a negation of the card's energies, but rather a rechanneling of its drive, a redirection of the projective impulse that typically characterizes the suit of Cups. The usual resolution and determination of the card are now mired in a sense of privation, a feeling of being stuck or blocked, unable to move forward.
