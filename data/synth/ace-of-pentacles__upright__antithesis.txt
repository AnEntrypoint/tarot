The Ace of Pentacles, a card of perfect contentment, embodies the unyielding drive of the material world, a force that resists the fluid, adaptive nature of the suit of Wands, with which it is paired. This card's solidity is a counterpoint to the airy, ethereal quality of the suit of Cups, and its groundedness a foil to the dreamy, imaginative realm of the suit of Swords.
