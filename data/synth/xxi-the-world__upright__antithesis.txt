In the dialectical dance of the tarot, XXI The World stands as the apotheosis of the journey, the culmination of the dialectical process. Yet, it is also the moment of greatest tension, the point at which the individual is poised between the certainties of the past and the unknowns of the future, the secure ground of the present moment slipping beneath their feet like sand. The World is the threshold of departure, the point of embarkation, the moment when the individual must take flight, leaving behind the familiar and embracing the uncharted.
