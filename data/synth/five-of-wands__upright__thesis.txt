In the midst of strife, the Five of Wands embodies the unyielding pursuit of wealth and opulence, a relentless competition for riches that knows no bounds, a battle of wills that is as much about the accumulation of material gain as it is about the validation of one's status. This is the card of the self-made man, driven by a singular focus on success in financial speculation, where the stakes are high and the rewards are few, and the only true measure of success is the one who emerges victorious, unscathed, and unrelenting.
