In the Seven of Swords, the figure stands at the precipice of a precipitous decision, poised between the certainties of counsel and the abyss of slander. The reversed position of this card suggests a collapse of the rational faculties, a surrender to the vacillating winds of opinion, and a descent into the mire of babbling, where good advice is lost in the din of unexamined thoughts. The once-clear voice of wisdom now falters, its words reduced to a mere echo of what was once a guiding principle.
