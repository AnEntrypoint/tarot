The Five of Cups, in its inverted state, bespeaks a stagnation of emotional currents, a damming of the flow that once coursed through the heart. The return of a relative, a figure from one's past, now blocked or turned inward, disrupts the equilibrium of the present, forcing a reevaluation of the lines of descent that have been drawn, the blood ties that bind, the ancestral affinities that once nourished the soul.
