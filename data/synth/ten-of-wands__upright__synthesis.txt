The Ten of Wands is a card of contradictions, its significances jarring and irreconcilable. On one hand, it signifies success and gain, the culmination of effort and striving, but on the other, it is a card of oppression, of burdens and difficulties that stifle and weigh upon the individual. The figure it depicts, burdened by the weight of its own ambitions, is often beset by the very things it has sought to achieve, its success turned sour by the perfidy of others or the corruption of its own desires.
