In the reversed VII Chariot, the force of will and determination, once a potent and forward-moving engine, is now stalled and turned inward, its momentum arrested by the weight of its own arrogance and pride. The charioteer, once a master of the waters, now finds itself trapped in a sea of self-importance and false glory, its vision obscured by the fog of its own presumption, its progress halted by the anchors of its own making.
