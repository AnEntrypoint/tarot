The Ace of Pentacles, as the primary source, embodies the synthesis of the reading, resolving the tensions and contradictions presented by the other cards. It is the culmination of the inquiry, the moment of perfect contentment and satisfaction, where the seeker's desires and the universe's will align, dissolving the dialectical oppositions that have been revealed. In this state of complete joy and delight, the seeker is one with the world, the root of Earth, the foundation of all being.
