In the inverted light of XVII The Star, the celestial harmony is disrupted, the music of the spheres reduced to a cacophony of discordant notes. The wind, once a gentle breeze, now howls like a hurricane, shattering the tranquility of the soul. The stars, once a guiding light, are obscured by the clouds of self-doubt and confusion, leaving the seeker lost in a sea of uncertainty, their own inner music silenced by the din of their own arrogance and haughtiness.
