In the Five of Wands, the tumult of strife and discord is turned inward, its energies now spent and exhausted, its sparks extinguished. The once-vibrant spark of conflict, now smothered by the weight of its own contradictions, lies dormant, its potential for creative expression and growth stifled. Yet, even in this state, the Five of Wands holds within it the seeds of its own resolution, a synthesis that absorbs the disparate elements, re-framing the chaos into a new, unified whole.
