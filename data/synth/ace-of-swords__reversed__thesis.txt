The Ace of Swords, in its upright position, asserts the clarity and precision of the rational mind, a sharp blade cutting through the chaos of the world. But in its reversed position, this same force is turned inward, its cutting edge dulled by self-doubt and indecision, the mind mired in its own confusion, unable to cut through the tangled web of its own thoughts, its conception of the world a jumbled mess of contradictions.
