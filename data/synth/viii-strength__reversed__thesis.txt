In the inverted VIII Strength, the force that once radiated outward with unyielding authority now falters, its power turned inward, crippled by the weight of its own despotism. The sovereign's dominion, once unchallenged, now teeters on the brink of collapse, its empire reduced to a mere shadow of its former self, its administration mired in weakness and discord, its very authority a hollow mockery of its former glory.
