In the reversed Page of Swords, the fluid, airy quality of the suit is congealed, its nimble, adaptive nature turned inward, fixated on its own reflections. The astonishing news that once brought such clarity and insight now lies mired in a morass of unpreparedness, the unforeseen becoming a source of sickness and disorientation. The earthy, grounded aspects of the personality, typically so adept at navigating the realm of ideas, are now crippled by a lack of foresight, their usual equilibrium disrupted by a sudden, ungrounding shock.
