The Knight of Cups embodies the dialectical synthesis of opposing forces, a union of opposing elements that yields a higher unity. As a harbinger of arrival, he brings together disparate threads, converging on a central point of resolution, where the tension between opposing forces is transcended. In this synthesis, the fluid, receptive nature of the Cups suit is ignited by the fiery, active quality of the Knight, yielding a union that is both transformative and harmonious.
