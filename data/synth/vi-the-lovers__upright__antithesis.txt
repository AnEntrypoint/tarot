In the dialectical dance of the Lovers, the union of opposites is not a harmonious resolution, but a tension that cannot be reconciled. The attraction and beauty of the card, with its promise of marriage and alliance, is undercut by the trials and nuptials that precede and follow it, a union that is as much a binding as it is a binding contract. The Lovers' meeting is a link, but also a collision, a collision of opposing forces that cannot be reconciled, a union that is as much a rupture as it is a joining.
