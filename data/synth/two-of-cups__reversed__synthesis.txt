In the Two of Cups, reversed, the fluid, receptive energies of the heart are congealed, turned inward, and rendered immobile. The desire, the yearning, the passion that once coursed through the veins like a river now stagnates, a blocked and fest'ring wound that refuses to heal. The will, the drive, the covetousness that once propelled the individual forward is now a self-referential, self-enclosed, and self-sustaining cycle of longing and frustration.
