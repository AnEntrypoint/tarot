The Knight of Cups in reverse is a figure of thwarted passion, his usual swashbuckling charm and guile now turned against himself, his subtlety and ruse rendered ineffectual, his artifice and duplicity exposed as weakness, his fire of water reduced to a smoldering ember, his usual suppleness and dexterity now clumsy and hampered, his very ability to navigate the complexities of human emotion turned against him, leaving him stranded in a sea of his own making.
