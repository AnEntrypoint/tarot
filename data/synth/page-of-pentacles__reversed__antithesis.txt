In the antithesis of the Page of Pentacles, the once-vigilant and industrious figure now languishes in a state of profligacy, their usual thriftiness and practicality giving way to a dissipated and squandering of resources. The earthy, grounded energy that once served as the foundation for their endeavors now seems to have turned against them, as if the very abundance and multiplicity they once cultivated has become a source of degradation and waste.
