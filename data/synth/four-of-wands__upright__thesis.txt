The Four of Wands suggests a state of perfected work, a domesticated and harmonious union of disparate elements, where the energies of the individual are directed towards a common goal, and the fruits of labor are reaped in a season of prosperity and peace. This is a moment of completion, where the fiery spark of creation has been tempered by the gentle touch of resolution, and the assembly of disparate parts has given rise to a cohesive whole, a union that is both strong and beautiful.
