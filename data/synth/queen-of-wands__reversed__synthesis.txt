In the Queen of Wands, reversed, we find a figure whose benevolent intentions are thwarted by circumstance, her good will unfulfilled by the absence of opportunity. This is not a card of compromise, but rather a card of arrested potential, where the spark of creativity and initiative is smothered by the weight of external constraints. The reversal of the Queen of Wands, then, is not a negation of her virtues, but rather their distortion, as if the very forces that once drove her forward are now turned inward, rendering her a shadow of her former self.
