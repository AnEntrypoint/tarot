In the King of Swords reversed, the rational faculties are crippled by their own ferocity, their sharpness turned inward to devour themselves. Cruelty, perversity, and perfidy writhe in the darkness, the malevolent energies of the intellect unmoored from their usual tether, unshackled from the moderating influence of compassion and justice. The result is a maelstrom of malice, a vortex of inhumanity that consumes all in its path, leaving naught but ruin and devastation.
