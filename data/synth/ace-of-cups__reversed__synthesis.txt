In the Ace of Cups, reversed, we find the tumult of the heart, its usual certainties upended. The fluid, receptive, and nurturing qualities of the suit of Cups are here blocked, their flow stymied, as if the very source of emotional life has been dammed. The root of Water, the element associated with the suit, is turned inward, its usual expansive, life-giving properties now constricted, its potential for growth and renewal stifled.
