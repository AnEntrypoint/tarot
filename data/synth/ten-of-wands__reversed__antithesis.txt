In the Ten of Wands, the weight of responsibility and duty presses upon us, a burden that threatens to crush the spirit. The reversal of this card, however, reveals a different kind of oppression - not the absence of weight, but the weight itself, turned inward, a self-imposed constraint that stifles the will. The contradiction between the desire for freedom and the necessity of constraint becomes a stifling, suffocating force, a constant reminder that our choices are not always our own.
