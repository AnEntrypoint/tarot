The Queen of Pentacles embodies a paradoxical fusion of opulence and restraint, her sumptuousness tempered by a hint of darkness, a shadow that underscores the tension between her assuredness and the fragility of her position. Waite's description of her as a "dark woman" suggests a complexity that belies her outward splendor, a sense of foreboding that underscores the instability of her seeming certitude.
