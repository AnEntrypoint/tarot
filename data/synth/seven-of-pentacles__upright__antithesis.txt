In the Seven of Pentacles, we find a card of paradoxical contradictions, its imagery beset by the tension between the opposing forces of gain and loss, of wealth and poverty, of purity and pollution. The silver and whiteness of the coin, a symbol of value and innocence, is sullied by the presence of altercation and quarrel, the dark undertow of conflict and strife that threatens to undermine the very notion of success and prosperity.
