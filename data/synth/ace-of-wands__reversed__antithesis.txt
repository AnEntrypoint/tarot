In the Ace of Wands, the fiery spark of creation and initiative is stifled, its radiance dimmed by the weight of its own potential. The force that once burst forth with unbridled energy now falters, its vitality siphoned off by the undertow of decay and ruin, leaving only a faint, flickering glow. This is not the birth of something new, but the decline of something old, the wasting away of a once-vibrant flame.
