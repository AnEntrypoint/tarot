The Queen of Cups, in her inverted state, bespeaks a woman whose virtues have curdled into vices, her refined sensibilities turned against themselves, her emotional depths now a mire of self-indulgence and corruption. Her once-noble aspirations, now thwarted and stifled, have given rise to a self-destructive impulse, a force that, like a poisoned well, drains the very lifeblood of her being.
