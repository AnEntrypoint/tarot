The Seven of Wands stands as a paragon of valour, its figure perched atop a mountain, unassailable despite the six adversaries arrayed against it. On the intellectual plane, this card signifies the cut and thrust of argument, the give-and-take of negotiation, the fiery passion of competition. Yet, it is also a card of success, for the one who holds this position has the upper hand, their opponents unable to reach them, their ascendancy unassailable.
