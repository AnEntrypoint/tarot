In the midst of calamity, the Tower stands as a stark, unyielding presence, its ruinous gaze piercing the very fabric of our being. Destitution and indigence, the twin specters of scarcity and need, haunt the landscape, their presence a constant reminder of the fragility of our existence. As the fiery element of Mars, the Tower reduces all pretensions to ashes, laying bare the stark, unvarnished truth of our most fundamental vulnerabilities.
