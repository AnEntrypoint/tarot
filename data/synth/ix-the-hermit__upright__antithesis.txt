The Hermit, a figure of calculated reserve, stands as the antithesis to the radiant light of the Fool, whose innocence and spontaneity are here tempered by a calculating gaze. This is the voice of prudence, where the line between truth and deception is constantly blurred, where the adept of the mysteries walks a tightrope between wisdom and treachery, where the very fabric of reality is woven from threads of duplicity and guile.
