The King of Pentacles embodies the synthesis of opposing forces, a union of the rational and the practical. He is the master of the material realm, a calculating and intelligent figure who navigates the world of commerce and science with ease, his intellectual prowess and business acumen rendering him a success in his chosen path. His dark, serious demeanor bespeaks a pragmatism that eschews the whimsical and the idealistic, instead embracing the concrete and the tangible.
