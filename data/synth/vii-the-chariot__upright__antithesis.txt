In the midst of turmoil, the Chariot stands firm, its forward motion unrelenting. The element of Water, associated with the Moon, underscores the fluid, ever-changing nature of the conflict, as the Chariot navigates the choppy waters of dissension, its will unbroken. Yet, the Chariot's unyielding resolve is also a source of trouble, its triumph a double-edged sword that cuts both ways, leaving in its wake a trail of discord and rebellion.
