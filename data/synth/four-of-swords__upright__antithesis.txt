In the Four of Swords, the figure of the hermitage is a paradoxical state of being both isolated and vigilant, a condition of exile and repose. The card's design, with its stark, unadorned landscape, suggests a tomb, a place of abandonment and solitude, yet also a site of introspection and quietude, where the self is forced to confront its own thoughts and desires. The card's association with Jupiter in Libra, a sign of balance and harmony, only heightens the tension, as the hermit's retreat from the world is both a refuge and a rejection, a truce with the self that is also a surrender to the void.
