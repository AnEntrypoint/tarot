The Queen of Cups embodies a paradoxical figure, oscillating between the virtues of a devoted and loving partner, and the duplicity of an equivocal character. Her tranquil surface, like the stillness of a moonlit lake, belies a depth that is at once both wise and treacherous, her virtues and vices inextricably linked, like the opposing forces of the zodiac's Water signs, Gemini and Cancer, where the fluidity of the former gives way to the nurturing intensity of the latter.
