In the Two of Wands, the dynamo of will and determination falters, its spark dimmed by the weight of its own contradictions. The fiery passion that once drove the aspirant forward now sputters, as the conflicting desires and impulses that once fueled its ascent now congeal into a paralyzing indecision. The once-clear path ahead is now shrouded in a haze of uncertainty, as the very drive to succeed is itself suffocated by the crushing burden of its own expectations.
