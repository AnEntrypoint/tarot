In the Tower's inverted stance, the fiery force of transformation, once unleashed upon the world, now turns inward, its flames reduced to smoldering embers. The element of fire, once a liberating force, now constricts, trapping the individual in a prison of their own making, a self-imposed captivity that stifles freedom and creativity. This is not a compromise, but a re-framing, a reorientation of the Tower's primal energy, now turned against itself, its power dissipated, its impact muted.
