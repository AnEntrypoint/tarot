In the crucible of crisis, the Tower stands as a monument to the unyielding force of upheaval, its flames consuming the complacent and the stagnant, casting them into the abyss of destitution and indigence. This is the card of unforeseen catastrophe, where the very foundations of our being are laid bare, and the certainties of our existence are reduced to ashes. The Tower's fiery wrath is the antithesis to the thesis of our carefully constructed lives, a force that shatters the illusions of our security and exposes the ruin that lies beneath.
