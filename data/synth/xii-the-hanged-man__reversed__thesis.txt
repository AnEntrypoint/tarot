In the inverted position of the Hanged Man, the usual suspension of the figure is disrupted, its limbs no longer stretched out in a gesture of sacrifice, but instead constricted, as if the body politic, that great, self-sustaining organism, has turned in upon itself, its selfishness and factional interests now a source of stasis and paralysis, the generation of ideas and progress blocked by the very forces that once drove them forward, the population reduced to a mere aggregation of isolated, disconnected units, the sovereign's will suborned by the petty squabbles of the crowd.
