The Seven of Pentacles presents a paradoxical figure, suspended between the material concerns of commerce and the spiritual aspirations of innocence. In one reading, it is a card of calculated gain, a ledger of transactions and the careful husbanding of resources, a pragmatic approach to the accumulation of wealth and status. Yet, another reading reveals a more troubled narrative, one of conflict and purification, a process of clearing away the impurities of a world that is often at odds with itself.
