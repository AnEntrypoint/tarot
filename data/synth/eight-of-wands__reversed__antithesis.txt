In the Eight of Wands, the arrow's trajectory is arrested, its trajectory deflected, its aim thwarted by the weight of its own momentum. The swift and decisive action that once propelled it forward now falters, its force turned inward, a source of internal agitation and regret, a querulous and querulous heart, a domestic turmoil that cannot be resolved, a misunderstanding that festers, a remorse that gnaws, a quarrel that cannot be quelled.
