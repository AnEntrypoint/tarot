In the inverted II The High Priestess, the veil of secrecy is rent asunder, revealing a garden of contradictions. The bubbling, fermentative energies that once coursed through the subconscious, now stagnate, trapped in a prison of self-doubt, where the effervescent waters of intuition have turned to a dull, stagnant pool. The moral fervor that once guided the seeker, now a conceit, a self-serving obsession with appearances, has eclipsed the true, unvarnished wisdom of the High Priestess, leaving only a hollow, surface knowledge in its wake.
