In the inverted realm of the Page of Cups, the tender and impressionable nature of this card is turned on its head, its usual flow of emotions and desires now a stagnant pool of bitter longing and unrequited passion. The once-soft spot of the heart, now hardened and calloused, is a magnet for the darker aspects of attachment, where seduction becomes deception and artifice, and the siren-song of love is replaced with a cacophony of unfulfilled yearning.
