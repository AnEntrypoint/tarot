In the Six of Wands, the triumphant figure stands poised on the threshold, yet in the reversed position, their victory is undercut by the weight of their own expectations. The gates of triumph, once open to the promise of success, now creak and groan with the treachery of delay, as the very forces that once propelled them forward now falter and stall. The fire that once burned with confidence and foresight now flickers with apprehension, as the selfsame energies that once fueled their ascent now turn inward, blocking the path to true fulfillment.
