In the inverted light of the Moon, the fluid, humid, and wave-like forces that once poured forth with unbridled abandon now stagnate and congeal, their deluge turned to desiccation, their inundation to drought. The once-vast, uncharted expanse of the subconscious, now a fetid and stagnant pool, reflects the instability and inconstancy that arises when the primal, watery element of the psyche is blocked and turned inward, its waves crashing against the walls of a self-imprisoned mind.
