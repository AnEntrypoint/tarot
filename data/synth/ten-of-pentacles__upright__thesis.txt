The Ten of Pentacles presents a scene of established order, a tableau of gain and riches that has been carefully cultivated through the careful management of resources and the prudent allocation of assets. This is a domain of settled values, where the value of a thing is determined by its provenance, its history, and its provenance is tied to the history of the family, the lineage, the bloodline. The very fabric of this reality is woven from the threads of tradition, of inheritance, and of the careful stewardship of the past.
