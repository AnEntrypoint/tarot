In the stark, unforgiving light of the Queen of Swords, the widow's isolation is a palpable, aching thing, a privation that seeps into every pore of her being, a sterility that cannot be alleviated by the absence of love or the scarcity of resources. Her withdrawal from the world is a self-imposed exile, a mourning for the life she once knew, and the sterility of her existence is a constant reminder of the fragility of human connection.
