In the inverted XX Judgement, the fiery spark of self-awareness, typically a clarion call to action, is instead muffled and stifled, its radiance diffused by the opacity of self-doubt. The strong, decisive voice of the Magus, now a mere whisper, is reduced to a hesitant murmur, its authority undermined by the weight of indecision. In this state of weakness, the individual is trapped in a cycle of self-doubt, unable to break free from the prison of their own indecisiveness.
