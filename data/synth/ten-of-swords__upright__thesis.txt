In the stark, unadorned image of the Ten of Swords, the weight of desolation is palpable, a tableau of pain and sorrow that bespeaks a world rent asunder by the ravages of time and human frailty. Waite's words - pain, affliction, tears, sadness - distill the essence of this card, a distillation that eschews the romanticism of more optimistic interpretations, instead embracing the bleakest of realities.
