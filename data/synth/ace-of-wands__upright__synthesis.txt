The Ace of Wands embodies the primal forces of creation and enterprise, the spark that ignites the passions and drives of individuals and endeavors. It is the source of all that is new and innovative, the principle that gives rise to the birth of ideas and the pursuit of fortune. Yet, it is also the root of calamities and catastrophes, the unbridled energy that can lead to destruction and chaos, as the unchecked flames of ambition and desire consume all in their path.
