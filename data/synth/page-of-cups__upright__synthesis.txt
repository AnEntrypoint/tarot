The Page of Cups embodies the dialectical synthesis of the suit, a union of the fluid, receptive qualities of the Water element with the practical, industrious energies of the Earth. This young man, studious and diligent, is driven to apply himself to the world, to render service and seek knowledge, his heart open to the currents of life, yet grounded in the solid, dependable realm of the practical. His reflection and meditation are not mere indulgences, but essential tools for navigating the ebbs and flows of the world, and finding the hidden patterns that underlie all things.
