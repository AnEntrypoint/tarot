The Two of Wands, in its inverted state, bespeaks a force of will that has been stymied, its dynamism turned inward, its energies squandered on self-doubt and indecision. This is not a lack of drive, but rather a misdirection of the will, a channeling of the primal energies of Mars in Aries into a maze of self-reflection and second-guessing, where the thrill of action and adventure is lost in a sea of what-ifs and maybes.
