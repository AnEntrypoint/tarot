In the Six of Cups, the past and its pleasures are inextricably linked, a nostalgic longing that arises from the ashes of what has vanished. Memories, like withered flowers, retain a faint scent of happiness, yet their beauty is tinged with the melancholy of loss. Yet, in this card, the past is not merely a relic of a bygone era, but a wellspring of new knowledge, a source of renewal that dissolves the tension between what was and what is, re-framing the present in a more nuanced light.
