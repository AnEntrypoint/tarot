In the shadowy realm of the Empress, the antithetical force of darkness gathers, an inscrutable presence that disrupts the radiant certainties of the day. Like the night that eclipses the sun, this opposing power subverts the Empress's own fecundity, her fruitfulness turned sterile, her initiative stymied by the unknown. The Empress's secrets are not just hidden, but actively concealed, her mysteries locked away from prying eyes, her very essence a cipher, unreadable, unknowable.
