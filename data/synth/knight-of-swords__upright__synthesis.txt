The Knight of Swords embodies the dialectical synthesis of opposing forces, its valor and bravery tempered by the sharpness of its sword, which cuts through the noise of enmity and opposition, revealing a higher truth. As a warrior of the mind, it navigates the battlefield of ideas, its skill and address allowing it to vanquish the forces of destruction and ruin, and emerge victorious, its fire of air igniting the spark of creation. In this synthesis, the tension between life and death, war and peace, is resolved, the knight standing as a symbol of the higher unity that absorbs both.
