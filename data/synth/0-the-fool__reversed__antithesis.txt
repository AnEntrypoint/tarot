In the inverted stance of the Fool, we behold a figure frozen in indecision, crippled by the very recklessness that once propelled it forward. The careless abandon of the upright position has given way to a stultifying self-absorption, as if the Fool's innate curiosity has curdled into a self-satisfied apathy. Its airy essence, once buoyant and free, now congeals into a leaden heaviness, weighed down by the nullity of its own potential.
