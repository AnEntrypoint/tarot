In the inverted state of the Page of Pentacles, the once-vigilant and industrious figure now succumbs to the very excesses of the material world, squandering its potential in a maelstrom of profligacy and waste. The earthy energies that once fueled its practicality now congeal into a hardened, unyielding obstinacy, as if the very abundance it once sought to harness has become a crushing burden, a weight that presses upon it with an unyielding force.
