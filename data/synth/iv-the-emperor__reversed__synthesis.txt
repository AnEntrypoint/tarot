In the inverted IV The Emperor, the stern figure of authority is beset by the very weaknesses he once embodied: benevolence curdled into obstruction, compassion warped into self-interest, and the shelter of protection turned inward, fortifying a fortress of isolation. The once-majestic figure now stumbles, his influence diminished by the very flaws that once sustained him, his charity turned to self-preservation, his defense a brittle, defensive shield.
