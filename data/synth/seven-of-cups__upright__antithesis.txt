In the realm of the Seven of Cups, the dialectical tension between the aspirant and the aspirant's desires is palpable. Waite's notion of "fairy favours" and "images of reflection" suggests a realm of possibility, but one that is fleeting and ephemeral, like the moon's phases. Meanwhile, the card's association with Venus in Scorpio, a sign of intense passion and transformation, implies a deeper, more profound resolution, one that is both alluring and treacherous, like the seductive power of the element Water.
