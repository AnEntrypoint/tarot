In the inverted, reversed, and thus weakened state of the XVIII Moon, the fluid, humid, and ever-changing nature of the element Water is turned inward, its waves and tides stilled, its deluge and inundation dammed. This is not a return to stability, but rather a collapse into the abyss of inconstancy, where the very ground beneath is silted and treacherous, where the lesser degrees of deception and error are not overcome, but rather, are the only reality.
