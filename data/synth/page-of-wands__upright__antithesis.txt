The Page of Wands, a young man of unbridled passion and unrelenting drive, embodies the contradictions of his suit: fiery and earthy, impulsive and calculating, a force of nature and a creature of reason. His impetuousness is tempered by a certain earthy pragmatism, a grounding that allows him to navigate the treacherous terrain of his own desires, even as he is drawn to the unknown, the uncharted, and the unexplored.
