In the Three of Swords, the querent's mental faculties are mired in a morass of confusion, their thoughts a jumble of error and distraction. This card is the antithesis to the clarity and balance of the Magician, the opposing force that cannot be resolved, a constant reminder of the limits of reason and the fragility of the ego. The querent's attempts at understanding are thwarted by the constant flux of their own thoughts, a self-perpetuating cycle of mental disarray.
