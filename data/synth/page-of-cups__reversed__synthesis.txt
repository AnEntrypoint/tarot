In the inverted realm of the Page of Cups, the fluid, emotional, and receptive qualities of the suit are constricted, as if the very forces that once drew the individual in with promises of attachment and affection now find themselves blocked or turned inward, unable to flow freely. The seductive power of the Page, once a potent force for enticement and persuasion, now withers, its artifice and cajolery reduced to a mere whisper, a faint echo of a once-deeper longing.
