In the Four of Cups, a card typically associated with the Moon in Cancer, we find a figure lost in contemplation, surrounded by the stagnant waters of the subconscious, where new knowledge or understanding, new directions, and new information are blocked, turned inward, and rendered inaccessible. The luxury of the Moon's gentle light, which normally illuminates the depths of the psyche, is here diminished, its rays diffused, and its promise of guidance and insight, muted.
