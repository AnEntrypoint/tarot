The Knight of Swords, in his reversed position, embodies the ignominious aspects of the suit, his usual valiant and decisive nature now crippled by his own ineptitude. His sharp intellect, typically a cutting force, is dulled by his own incompetence, rendering him ineffectual in the face of even the most trivial disputes, particularly with those who are themselves imbeciles, or with women who are his rivals, whom he will ultimately be unable to conquer.
