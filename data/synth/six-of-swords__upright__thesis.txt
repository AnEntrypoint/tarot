The voyage, a journey by water or route, is a means to an end, a way to arrive at a destination, a step on the path, a promenade through the medium of life. Waite's words, a synthesis of Etteilla's, suggest a pleasant, even expedient, passage, a career of sorts, a delivery of self into the world. In this sense, the Six of Swords is not merely a card of departure, but a card of navigation, charting the course of one's life, guided by the airy, intellectual currents of Mercury in Aquarius, ever-seeking the optimal route, the most expedient path.
