The Ace of Cups, a card of unshakeable resolve, asserts the primacy of the heart's desires, a fixed and unalterable law that governs the flow of emotions and experiences. In this tableau of the soul, the heart's table is set with the abundance of life, a feast of love and nourishment that invites the self to partake in the banquet of the world, a place of hospitality where the guest is always welcome.
