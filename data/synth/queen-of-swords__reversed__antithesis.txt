In the Queen of Swords reversed, malice and deceit seep into the very fabric of reason, tainting the clarity of thought with a subtle artifice that masquerades as objectivity. The once-noble pursuit of truth becomes a means to an end, a tool for manipulation and control, as the querent's own will is subverted by the insidious influence of a bad woman, whose ill-will towards them is a palpable force, a malignity that cannot be ignored.
