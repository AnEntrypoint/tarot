In the synthesis of XX Judgement, the dialectical tension between opposing forces is resolved, yielding a higher unity that absorbs both. This is not a compromise, but a re-framing in which the opposing elements are re-imagined, their differences sublated into a new, transcendent whole. The card's association with the Hebrew letter Shin, the element of Fire, and the planet Pluto, suggests a fiery, transformative process that burns away the old, revealing a new, radiant truth.
