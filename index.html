Three Cards — a literary tarot.

Hegelian spread: thesis — antithesis — synthesis. Every meaning is verbatim or transcribed from a primary source — Waite's Pictorial Key (1910), Etteilla's Manière de se récréer (1785), and the Golden Dawn attribution table. No interpolation, no generated prose.

Each card is drawn uniformly at random across all 156 (78 cards × 2 orientations). All readings are pre-generated and served as static files; no LLM runs at draw-time. There are 474,552 distinct three-card readings in the system.
